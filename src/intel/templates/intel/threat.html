How to read these numbers (and why they can differ from zkillboard.com or other sources)

lscan reads raw zKillboard killmail dumps (no live zKillboard API calls) and gets pilot identity from ESI. lscan counts pure PvP only: a player must have killed a player, so ratting losses and structure kills never appear. Pods fold into the ship kill before them. Stealth hulls (covert ops cloak, or immunity to directional scan) are highlighted on purpose.
